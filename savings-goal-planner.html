Savings and planning

Turn a savings goal into a monthly plan.

Estimate how an initial balance, regular contributions, time, and a chosen annual return could add up. Change one assumption at a time to see how the projection moves.

Open the savings goal calculator →

Start with the goal and date

Write down what you are saving for, how much you may need, and when you expect to need it. A target date can help you choose a realistic contribution amount and review the plan as circumstances change. The Financial Consumer Agency of Canada guide to savings and investment goals explains how to set a goal and make a plan.

What the calculator compares

  • Starting amount: savings already set aside for the goal.
  • Monthly contribution: a regular amount added at the end of each month.
  • Goal amount and timeline: the target balance and the number of years to save.
  • Estimated annual return: a scenario assumption converted to an equivalent monthly rate for the projection.

The result shows a projected ending balance, total contributions, estimated growth, and an approximate monthly contribution needed to reach the target under the selected assumptions.

Returns are uncertain

A fixed return is a simplifying assumption, not a forecast. Actual investment returns vary and can be negative. Fees, taxes, inflation, contribution timing, and the type of account or investment can also affect the amount available. Try several return assumptions, including a lower-return case, instead of treating one projection as the expected outcome.

For an emergency fund or a near-term purchase, consider how much fluctuation you can accept and when the money must be available. This calculator does not recommend an account, investment, or contribution amount.

Questions to revisit

  • Is the goal amount stated in today's dollars, or should it account for future price increases?
  • Can the monthly contribution fit alongside essential expenses and debt payments?
  • Will the timeline or amount need to change if income, costs, or returns differ from the scenario?
