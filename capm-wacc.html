How the estimate works

CAPM cost of equity = risk-free rate + beta × expected market risk premium. Beta represents the stock's sensitivity to market returns in the chosen estimation period.

WACC = equity weight × cost of equity + debt weight × pre-tax cost of debt × (1 − tax rate). The capital weights should reflect a defensible market-value financing mix. The result is only as useful as its assumptions.

Use a matching discount rate

WACC is commonly paired with free cash flow to the firm. Cash flow to equity is generally discounted using cost of equity instead. Keep cash flows, currency, inflation basis, and discount-rate assumptions consistent.

Continue to the DCF valuation model to use a discount rate in a cash-flow valuation.

For education only. CAPM and WACC depend on estimation choices. This calculator does not estimate beta, market returns, debt value, or a security's fair value.
