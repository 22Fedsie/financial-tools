How the estimates work

Methods, assumptions, and limits.

Each result is calculated from values entered and simplified assumptions. Change an assumption and the result can change. Actual costs, tax treatment, returns, and lender terms may differ.

Mortgage payment

Uses the entered principal, annual rate, and amortization. The rate converts from Canadian semi-annual compounding to a monthly rate, then uses the standard fixed-payment formula. It assumes the rate and schedule stay unchanged and excludes property tax, insurance, mortgage default insurance, fees, and other ownership costs.

Investment growth and savings goal

Converts the effective annual return to an equivalent monthly rate. The balance grows monthly and contributions are added at month end. It estimates the contribution needed for the selected savings goal. Returns are hypothetical and exclude fees, taxes, inflation, and changes in returns.

Open the investment calculator →

Debt payoff

Divides the annual interest rate by 12, rounds monthly interest and payments to cents, and pays the balance down with a fixed monthly payment. The final payment is limited to the remaining amount. Lenders may calculate interest daily or apply payments differently; fees are excluded.

Open the debt calculator →

Rent versus buy

Projects home value from the entered annual change, subtracts mortgage balance and estimated selling costs, and compares owner equity with a renter's investment portfolio. The less expensive monthly housing scenario invests the difference. Rent increases yearly; owner costs stay fixed. Tax effects, repairs beyond the input, mortgage insurance, and other costs are excluded. This is not a recommendation.

Open the rent-versus-buy calculator →

RRSP versus TFSA

Compares the same take-home budget invested monthly. TFSA contributions equal the budget. RRSP contributions are grossed up at the entered current marginal rate, assuming the full deduction is reinvested, then estimated withdrawal tax is applied. It assumes rates stay unchanged and excludes limits, fees, account rules, and individual tax details. See the CRA RRSP information and CRA TFSA information.

Open the RRSP-versus-TFSA calculator →

Net worth

Adds entered asset amounts and subtracts entered debts. Values are not verified and anything omitted is not counted.
