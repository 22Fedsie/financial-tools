About this project

Clearer numbers for everyday money decisions.

Financial Tools is a growing collection of free, practical calculators for people in Canada who want to explore a financial question and understand the assumptions behind an estimate.

What you can do here

Explore mortgage payments, investment growth, debt payoff, housing scenarios, registered-account estimates, portfolio returns, and valuation models. The calculators are designed to make it easier to compare scenarios and prepare questions for your own research.

The Money basics page adds plain-language guidance. Interactive models cover portfolio returns, multi-stock portfolio analysis, CAPM and WACC, DCF valuation, relative valuation, and Black-Scholes.

How to use the estimates

Change the example inputs to fit a scenario you want to explore. Read the notes shown beside each result, and review the calculator methodology for the assumptions and limitations. Results are illustrations, not promises or personal recommendations.

For education and planning. These tools do not replace personalized financial, mortgage, investment, or tax advice. Consider a qualified professional for decisions that depend on your circumstances.

Privacy

The site does not collect calculator inputs, email addresses, or visitor feedback. It does not use Cloudflare Web Analytics. See the privacy page for details about browser processing and hosting.
