Your privacy

Simple tools, no email signup.

Updated October 2, 2026

Calculator information

Calculator inputs and results are processed in your browser. This site does not send those values to a calculator service or save them in an account. Do not use the tools as a secure place to store financial records.

No email or feedback collection

There is no newsletter, email signup, account, feedback form, advertising tracker, or analytics service on the site. Cloudflare Web Analytics is not enabled. No personal email address is displayed or collected.

Website hosting

The site is hosted by Cloudflare Pages. Like other web hosts, Cloudflare may process technical request information such as IP address, browser details, and requested pages to deliver and protect the website. See Cloudflare's privacy policy. Links to outside sites, including government resources and GitHub, are governed by those sites' privacy information.

Public feedback is paused

The feedback page does not accept messages, and the website does not collect voluntary feedback. Do not share private financial details or account credentials through public websites.

For learning and planning. This page describes the current site. Hosting and website features may change; this page should be updated if data collection is introduced.
