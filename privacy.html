Your privacy

Simple tools, no email signup.

Updated October 1, 2026

Calculator information

The calculator inputs and results are processed in your browser. This site does not send those values to a calculator service or save them in an account. Do not use the tools as a secure place to store financial records.

No email collection

There is no newsletter, email signup, account, advertising tracker, or analytics service on the site at this time. No personal email address is displayed or collected.

Website hosting

The site is hosted by Cloudflare Pages. Like other web hosts, Cloudflare may process technical request information such as IP address, browser details, and requested pages to deliver and protect the website. See Cloudflare's privacy policy for its handling practices. Links to outside sites, including government resources and GitHub, are governed by those sites' privacy information.

Feedback

The current feedback option opens a public GitHub issue. Issues may be visible to anyone and require a GitHub account. Please do not include your email, account details, personal information, or private financial figures. See GitHub's privacy statement. This site does not offer a private contact channel yet; it will be added before any email or other personal information is collected.

Questions or privacy concerns? Please do not post private details in public feedback. A private contact option will be provided before the site collects personal information.
