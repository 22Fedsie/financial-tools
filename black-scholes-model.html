The model assumes a European-style option, continuous rates and dividend yield, and constant volatility over the option's life.

What the model represents

A call gives its holder the right to buy at the strike; a put gives the right to sell. The model estimates a theoretical value under simplifying assumptions. It is not a forecast of the option's future market price.

Higher volatility and more time generally increase the value of a plain call or put, while rates, dividends, and whether the option is a call or put affect the result differently.

Important limitations

Real options may be American-style and exercise can occur before expiration. Volatility, interest rates, dividends, transaction costs, liquidity, taxes, and market conditions may change. The model does not estimate implied volatility or account for early exercise, discrete dividends, or changing volatility.

For education only. Options can lose value rapidly and may involve substantial risk. This model is not a trading signal or a recommendation to buy or sell an option.
